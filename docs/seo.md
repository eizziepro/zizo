# Zizo Surrogacy — SEO, AEO and GEO Plan

**Position in document sequence:** 2 of 7. This plan takes its audience paths and calls to action from `userflow.md`. It defines the page architecture and discoverability requirements that `schema.md`, `trd.md`, `design.md`, `design-build.md` and the implementation plan must preserve.

## Search positioning

The first search market is Lagos, Nigeria. The primary audiences are intended parents and surrogate applicants. Treat the keyword themes below as hypotheses for page planning, not as verified search-volume data or promised rankings. Validate them after launch with Search Console and, if available, keyword research data.

Initial intent themes include “surrogacy agency Lagos,” “surrogacy in Lagos,” “surrogacy in Nigeria,” “surrogacy for intended parents,” “become a surrogate in Lagos,” “surrogate support Nigeria,” and “how surrogacy works in Nigeria.” Use “Nigeria” or any additional city only on pages that accurately describe where Zizo actually serves people. Create an egg-donation page only if that service is confirmed for the website release.

Google recommends useful, clear, unique and people-first content, descriptive page organization and crawlable internal links. Search changes may take time, and following best practices cannot guarantee indexing or a particular ranking. [1]

## Search-focused page structure

The user flow in `userflow.md` becomes this proposed public URL structure:

- `/` — Zizo’s verified service, Lagos market and the two audience paths.
- `/intended-parents/` — the intended-parent service, process, boundaries and booking path.
- `/become-a-surrogate/` — the surrogate enquiry path and accurate first-contact information.
- `/how-surrogacy-works/` — a carefully reviewed overview of the process and Zizo’s role.
- `/about/` — the organization, named team members, experience and contact identity.
- `/book-a-call/` — appointment type, duration, availability and scheduling.
- `/contact/` — telephone, WhatsApp, address only if appropriate to publish, and response expectations.
- `/insights/` and `/insights/{article-slug}/` — the Agency Insights listing and individual articles.
- `/egg-donation/` — include only if the offering is active, approved and distinct enough to deserve a useful page.

The earlier live-site audit found a single-page experience and a sitemap containing only the homepage. Before launch, recheck the current URLs and make the new sitemap include every canonical, indexable page. A sitemap can support discovery but does not guarantee crawling or indexing. [5] [9]

## Page-level SEO brief

Give every indexable page one clear search intent and one human audience. Write a distinct title, H1 and introduction. Keep the introduction direct enough to answer “what does Zizo do and who is this for?” before the visitor reaches long explanatory copy. Use descriptive headings, internal links to the next step and visible contact routes. Do not copy the same generic paragraph across multiple pages.

Suggested title directions, to finalize after service claims are verified:

- Home: **Surrogacy Support in Lagos, Nigeria | Zizo Surrogacy**
- Intended parents: **Surrogacy Support for Intended Parents in Lagos | Zizo**
- Surrogate applicants: **Surrogate Enquiries in Lagos, Nigeria | Zizo Surrogacy**
- Process: **How Surrogacy Works in Nigeria | Zizo Surrogacy**
- Booking: **Book a Call with Zizo Surrogacy**

The page title and snippet should describe the actual page, not promise rank, approval, pregnancy, a baby, legal outcomes, or a guaranteed timeline. Use one clear H1 per page and descriptive internal-link text rather than repeated “Learn More.” Google can generate titles and snippets differently from the supplied metadata. [1]

## Content and trust plan

Surrogacy involves high-stakes personal, medical and legal questions. Publish only claims the organization can substantiate. For relevant content, identify the writer and any qualified reviewer, describe their real credentials accurately, distinguish Zizo’s coordination role from clinical or legal advice, cite reliable primary sources, and show when a page was materially updated. Obtain permission for identifiable stories, quotes and photos. Never invent outcomes, screening claims, medical expertise, success rates or partner affiliations. Google’s people-first guidance emphasizes clear purpose, real expertise and helpful content rather than pages written mainly to attract search traffic. [6]

Build useful content around real visitor questions, such as the steps in the process, what a first call covers, what information is needed to book, how to contact the team privately, and which matters must be discussed with a qualified clinician or lawyer. FAQ sections can serve visitors, but do not promise an FAQ search enhancement: Google removed its FAQ rich-result feature in May 2026. [7]

## Local SEO for Lagos

If Zizo is eligible for a Google Business Profile, claim and verify it. Keep the real-world business name, supported category, service details, hours, telephone and any public-facing address accurate and consistent. Add current, genuine photos and respond professionally to reviews. Google describes local results as primarily based on relevance, distance and prominence; complete, accurate profile information helps Google understand the business. No one can pay or request a guaranteed local ranking. [2]

Do not publish a made-up address, use a virtual office as a storefront, add city keywords to the business name, or create thin pages for Lagos neighbourhoods Zizo does not serve. If the team does not receive visitors at a physical location, confirm Business Profile eligibility and address-display rules before setting a public address. Use the same approved name, phone and web address across legitimate organization profiles and directories.

## Technical SEO requirements

The custom-coded build should provide:

- Crawlable, server-rendered or statically rendered core text, links and headings. Do not rely on animation, image text or client-side interaction as the only place where important content appears.
- One canonical URL per public page, human-readable paths, HTTPS, working internal links and redirects from any replaced legacy URL.
- A `robots.txt` that does not accidentally block important pages, CSS or JavaScript, plus an XML sitemap containing canonical indexable URLs only.
- Search Console verification, sitemap submission and URL inspection for launch templates. The sitemap informs Google about important URLs but is not an indexing guarantee. [5]
- Compressed responsive images with meaningful alt text when informative, explicit image dimensions, reduced-motion support and no large blank/loading areas that delay the core message.
- Core Web Vitals monitoring. Google’s current “good” thresholds are LCP at or below 2.5 seconds, INP below 200 milliseconds and CLS below 0.1 at the 75th percentile of real visits. [4]

Use descriptive page titles, visible page text, accessible navigation and sensible internal links as the foundation. Google says there are no extra technical requirements or special AI-only markup for a page to be eligible as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode; pages still need to be indexed and eligible to appear with a snippet. [3]

## AEO and GEO: practical approach

Treat answer-engine visibility as a result of useful, well-structured, trustworthy web pages—not a separate hidden content format. On each service and article page, answer the main question in plain language, define uncommon terms, label the author/reviewer accurately, show relevant dates and sources, and link to the page that explains the next step. Keep important content accessible to crawlers and human visitors. Do not add fabricated “AI-optimized” quotations, unsupported facts, hidden text or keyword blocks. Google says special AI files and special schema are not required for AI Overviews or AI Mode. [3]

Measure Google search clicks, impressions, CTR, queries and pages in Search Console. Google reports AI-feature visits as part of the overall Web search performance reporting, rather than as a separate AI-only traffic metric. [3] [8]

Bing Webmaster Tools’ AI Performance preview can provide an additional view of citation activity. Its June 2026 preview describes intent and topic groupings, citation share and period comparison. Microsoft describes citation share as observational, not a rank score or traffic share. Use this as a secondary visibility signal, not as a substitute for enquiries, confirmed calls or search traffic. [10]

## Measurement and operating rhythm

Before launch, record the current Search Console baseline and confirm that conversion events from `userflow.md` do not include personal information. Review Search Console by query, page, country, device and date. Watch impressions and clicks for Lagos/Nigeria intent, organic visits to audience pages, appointment starts and confirmed calls, WhatsApp clicks and article-assisted conversions. Search Console’s average position is an aggregate metric; an individual search can vary by time, location, device and history. [8]

Review the first four weeks for indexing and technical errors. After a sufficient data period, improve pages that receive relevant impressions but weak clicks or that attract visitors who do not find the expected information. Refresh content when the underlying service, contact details or reliable source guidance changes. Do not judge AEO/GEO performance from a single prompt or one manually observed answer.
