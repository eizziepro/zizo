Design and build the website "Zizo Donor and Surrogacy — A safe fertility brand that creates families across borders" end to end as a multi-page Tailwind CSS and Vanilla JavaScript site with a Supabase backend, including a booking system, a review system, a blog CMS and an admin dashboard.

The section flow, motion language, layering and code conventions follow the "Visual Production Studio" template, adapted for the fertility, surrogacy and donor-care industry. The result must match this specification, not a generic fertility-clinic template and not a film-studio look. The tone is calm, warm, premium, private and trustworthy.

Preserve source quirks:
- The copyright year is explicitly set to 2026: "© 2026 Zizo Donor and Surrogacy. All rights reserved."
- The "Surrogacy" core service item uses a distinct project index fraction "01 / 04", while the others use simple integers like "02", "03", "04".
- The Process section uses a unique teal variation for the "A clear route to parenthood" heading text: #12504C.
- Testimonial attributions keep the original anonymised style: "Parent AJ", "Parent SS & MV", "B.O., Surrogate Mother, Lagos".
- Original calls to action keep their wording: "Start Your Journey", "Message us", "Interested in becoming a surrogate?", "Book a Consultation".
- The brand name is "Zizo" (short: "Zizo Surrogacy"). Never "Zizulu".

CRITICAL FIDELITY CONSTRAINTS
- The global background is strictly #FAF9F6 (Warm White). The site is NOT a 100% dark theme. Charcoal #17191A is used only for: the footer, the reviews/testimonials band, the scrolled-state text, and hero/image overlays.
- Core brand colours are authoritative and must never change: Charcoal Black #17191A (primary), Warm White #FAF9F6 (secondary), Deep Teal #17645F (accent). Tints and shades are supporting colours only and must never compete with the core three. No other accent colours anywhere.
- Do not add any hover scaling to text, buttons or navigation links; only the image containers in the Core Services and Surrogate bands should scale.
- The hero image must use the exact specific linear-gradient overlay: linear-gradient(to top, rgba(23,25,26,.88), rgba(23,25,26,.10) 55%, rgba(23,25,26,.42)).
- Transition for the "reveal" class must be exactly 700ms cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1).
- Header navigation has a maximum of 5 items excluding Home (logo), plus one CTA button. Dropdowns have a maximum of 4 items each.
- Fonts are strictly Fraunces (headings) and Hanken Grotesk (body, nav, buttons, forms). Never use Inter, Arial, Poppins, Lato, Roboto, Manrope or Dela Gothic One.
- All font sizes, section padding, gaps, hero height, radii and button padding use the clamp() tokens defined below. No ad-hoc pixel values except 1px borders and form inputs (fixed 16px).
- Never fabricate statistics, outcomes, success rates, legal claims, guarantees, awards, testimonials or team members. Anything unknown is a placeholder token such as {{FAMILIES_HELPED}} stored in site_settings and flagged for client confirmation.
- Never show real names, faces or identifying details of surrogates, donors or parents. Reviews show initials or "Parent AJ" style labels only.
- Do not use AI-generated faces next to testimonials or reviews, and never caption AI imagery as real clients.

BOOKING SYSTEM (developed alongside the build; the header CTA, footer link, service pages and blog posts all link to /book/)
- Flow (4 steps with a progress indicator): 1 Choose a consultation type, 2 Pick a date and time, 3 Your details, 4 Confirm. Works fully on mobile and by keyboard.
- Consultation types (editable in admin): "Intended Parent Consultation" (30 min), "Surrogate Information Call" (20 min), "Egg Donor Information Call" (20 min), "Cost and Program Planning" (45 min). Each has duration, buffer after, channel options (Google Meet, phone call, WhatsApp call), price (default free) and an intro text.
- Calendar: custom vanilla JS month view with available days highlighted in a teal tint, time slots listed for the selected day, and the visitor's local timezone auto-detected and displayed alongside Africa/Lagos (WAT). Slots are generated server-side so the browser never decides availability.
- Availability rules: weekly working hours per weekday, date exceptions (holidays, leave), minimum notice (default 12 hours), booking horizon (default 60 days), slot interval, buffer, and daily booking cap.
- Details form fields: full name, email, phone or WhatsApp number, country, "I am an" (Intended Parent, Surrogate, Egg Donor, Other), preferred channel, short note (helper text: "Please do not include medical details here."), consent checkbox for contact and privacy, Turnstile.
- Double-booking prevention: Postgres exclusion constraint on a tstzrange per staff member (btree_gist), and a `create-booking` Edge Function that re-validates the slot, inserts atomically, and returns a friendly "that time was just taken" message with refreshed slots on conflict.
- Statuses: pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled, no_show. Default is confirmed automatically; an admin toggle switches to "request, then approve".
- Notifications: confirmation email to the visitor with an .ics calendar file and the manage link; alert email (and optional WhatsApp Business API or click-to-chat alert) to the admin; reminders at 24 hours and 1 hour before via pg_cron and an Edge Function; follow-up email after the consultation with a link to the verified review form (see REVIEW SYSTEM) and the next-step CTA.
- Manage link: signed, single-purpose token URL to reschedule or cancel, limited to {{CANCEL_CUTOFF_HOURS}} hours before the start. Never expose booking IDs or personal data in URLs.
- Optional Google Calendar sync and automatic Meet link creation via the Google Calendar API (service account or OAuth), behind a settings toggle.
- Optional payment step (off by default): Paystack for NGN and Stripe for other currencies, with the booking confirmed only after payment success via webhook.
- Admin side: calendar and list views, filters, manual booking, reschedule, cancel, internal notes, CSV export, and block-out times.
- Edge cases: daylight-saving and timezone differences, past dates disabled, double-submit protection, rate limiting (max 5 attempts per IP per hour), and graceful offline and error states.

REVIEW SYSTEM
- Submission: a public "Write a review" form on /resources/stories-and-reviews/ and a verified-link form at /review/submit/?token= sent after a completed consultation or journey milestone. Fields: display name (default "Parent AJ" style initials), role (Intended Parent, Surrogate, Egg Donor), star rating 1 to 5 (accessible radio group), review title, review text (50 to 1,500 characters), country (optional), consent checkboxes ("I agree Zizo may publish this review" and "I understand my review will be shown anonymously"), Turnstile, honeypot field.
- Anonymity: reviews are never shown with full names, photos or identifying details. Edit or redact any identifying content during moderation.
- Moderation: every review is saved as `pending`. Admin can approve, edit (with an edit log), reject, feature (shown on Home) or archive. Verified reviews (from a token) get a "Verified client" label.
- Admin reply: optional public reply from Zizo shown beneath a review.
- Display: Home carousel, /resources/stories-and-reviews/ list with filters and pagination, and per-service pages showing 2 to 3 relevant reviews. Average rating computed from approved reviews. Hide the average until at least 5 approved reviews exist.
- Anti-abuse: one review per verified token, rate limiting, spam and profanity filter, duplicate detection, link blocking in review text.
- Testimonial import: seed the three source testimonials as approved, featured, `source = 'legacy'`, and flag them "confirm permission to publish" in admin.

SECURITY, PRIVACY AND COMPLIANCE
- Treat the site as handling sensitive personal data. Enforce HTTPS, HSTS, a strict Content-Security-Policy (allow only the CDNs listed, Supabase, Turnstile and the analytics host), X-Frame-Options DENY, Referrer-Policy strict-origin-when-cross-origin, and Permissions-Policy that disables unused features.
- Compliance targets: the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and GDPR for visitors from the EU and UK. Provide explicit consent capture, a data retention policy, a data-subject request path (access, correction, deletion) through the contact form, and a breach-response contact. Policies are drafted as templates and flagged {{LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED}}.

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
- Do not use Tailwind's default duration or easing for the reveal; use the custom cubic-bezier.
- Do not forget the `scale-[1.03]` on the hero background container to prevent edge gaps during parallax.
- Do not use a generic plus/minus for FAQ; use the rotating Lucide `plus` icon.
- Do not make the site fully dark. Only the footer and the reviews band are Charcoal.
- Do not use teal tints for text or links, and do not use Deep Teal text on Charcoal.
- Do not use glossy, airbrushed or plastic-looking skin in any generated image, and do not use people outside the specified identities.
- Do not use more than 5 header nav items or more than 4 items per dropdown.
- Do not put medical details, IDs or photos into public forms.
- Do not let the browser decide booking availability or write directly to protected tables.
- Do not render unconfirmed stats, prices, timelines or legal claims. Use placeholders and the admin flag.
- Do not render AI faces beside testimonials or call AI images real clients.
- Do not leave the CDN Tailwind build in production.
